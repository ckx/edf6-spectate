# EDF6 Spectate 0.3.0 experimental

Adds a smaller spectator HUD:

- The watched teammate's native health bar, with current and maximum health.
- Native multiplayer name/health indicators, including the watched teammate.
  Names follow the game's display settings.

The health widget reads the watched player directly. The multiplayer indicators
keep your local player as their viewer. The full local-player HUD builder is not
called, and spectator cameras get no ammo, radar, damage effects or class gauges.
Your own HUD returns with your camera on revival.

The code was checked against the supported EDF.dll and cross-compiled. This HUD
has not been verified online. Version 0.2.1 remains the camera-only fallback.
No CI or new test suite was added. Runtime faults now log their instruction address
and setup logs identify which component was being created.

Close EDF6, back up saves and extract the ZIP to a separate folder. Run
`INSTALL.bat` on Windows or `bash install.sh` on Linux. Existing loaders, mods and
launch settings are preserved.

In an online mission, go down and watch your teammate lose/recover health. Check
names and health values, then get revived and confirm that your own HUD returns.
Repeat the death/revive cycle. Retain the spectator and crash logs if it fails.
