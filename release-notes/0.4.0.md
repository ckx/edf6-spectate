# EDF6 Spectate 0.4.0 experimental

While you are down online, the spectator camera now orbits the watched teammate
and points where you look. Turn it with your normal look controls (mouse or right
stick, with your own bindings and sensitivity). It no longer copies the
teammate's aim.

How it works: each frame the plugin sets the game's own orbit mode on the watched
teammate's follow camera and gives it your look pitch and yaw. The game's normal
camera offsets and smoothing still apply. Nothing is sent to other players, and
your own character and camera are not changed.

Known and expected behavior:

- Static analysis says the game hides the separate health bar while a camera is in
  this mode. The name/health indicators above players should stay. Not verified.
- A teammate revive or some state changes can show the teammate's own view for a
  single frame.
- There is no detached free-fly camera and no button to switch teammates.

Tested: built with clang for MinGW-w64; the four existing tests passed under Wine
against the game's EDF.dll, including the install check over the new code ranges.
The new ranges also matched a running game, read only. Not tested in an online
room. Version 0.3.0 remains the fallback.

Supported: Windows and Linux (Proton), Steam EDF.dll from the January 2025 update.

Close EDF6, back up saves and extract the ZIP to a separate folder. Run
`INSTALL.bat` on Windows or `bash install.sh` on Linux. Existing loaders, mods and
launch settings are preserved.

In an online mission, go down, look around your teammate with the mouse or right
stick and get revived. Report whether the view turns, whether the health bar and
indicators show, and whether your own camera returns normally. Keep
`Mods/Plugins/EDF6Spectate.log` and any crash log if something goes wrong.
